How the Calculation Works

PocketDose applies straightforward arithmetic to the values you enter. There is no AI, no server, and no hidden logic. Every formula is shown here so you can verify the result yourself.

Step 1 — Carb Portions (CPs)

For each food item you enter a weight (grams) and carbohydrate content (grams per 100 g). The app calculates CPs using the standard UK convention where 1 CP = 10 g of carbohydrate:

Carbs (g) = Weight (g) × Carbs per 100 g ÷ 100
CPs = Carbs (g) ÷ 10
Total CPs = sum of CPs across all items in the meal

No food database is consulted. The carb values are always the ones you type in.

Step 2 — Meal Dose

Your insulin-to-carb ratio (set in Settings) converts Total CPs into units of insulin:

Meal Dose (units) = Total CPs × Insulin-to-Carb Ratio (units per CP)

Step 3 — Correction Dose

If your current blood glucose differs from your target, a correction is added using your correction factor (also set in Settings):

Correction Dose (units) = (Current BG − Target BG) ÷ Correction Factor

A negative result (BG below target) reduces the total dose. If BG is at or below 4 mmol/L the app will not produce a dose result — you must treat the hypo first.

Step 4 — CGM Trend Adjustment

If you select a CGM trend arrow, the app applies a percentage adjustment to the meal dose (not a fixed number of units). A rising glucose means you may need slightly more insulin for the same meal; a falling glucose means slightly less. Ultra-fast insulins (Fiasp, Lyumjev) use half the standard percentage because their faster action profile already accounts for some of the trend:

Trend Adjustment (units) = Meal Dose × Trend Percentage
CGM Arrow Direction Standard insulin Ultra-fast insulin
↑↑Rising rapidly+20% of meal dose+10% of meal dose
↑Rising+15% of meal dose+7.5% of meal dose
↗Rising slightly+10% of meal dose+5% of meal dose
→Steadyno adjustmentno adjustment
↘Falling slightly−10% of meal dose−5% of meal dose
↓Falling−15% of meal dose−7.5% of meal dose
↓↓Falling rapidly−20% of meal dose−10% of meal dose

If no trend arrow is selected, this adjustment is zero.

Total Dose

The three components are summed, clamped so the result cannot be negative, then rounded to the nearest whole unit:

Raw Total = Meal Dose + Correction Dose + Trend Adjustment
Clamped = max(0, Raw Total)
Final Dose = round(Clamped) — nearest whole unit

The app shows the final dose alongside a full breakdown of each component so you can verify every part of the calculation before acting on it.
