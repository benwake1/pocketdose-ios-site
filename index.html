What it does

🍽️

Carb Calculator

Enter portion sizes and carbs per 100g — PocketDose calculates your total carb portions (CPs) instantly. Save favourite meals as presets.

💉

Dose Calculation

Combines your meal dose, blood glucose correction, and CGM trend adjustment into a single clear result with a full breakdown.

📈

CGM Trend Support

Seven CGM arrow directions, each with an evidence-based dose adjustment. Ultra-fast insulins (Fiasp, Lyumjev) automatically use halved adjustments.

⚙️

Your Settings

Set your own insulin-to-carb ratio, correction factor, target BG, insulin type, and concentration. All saved locally on your device.

📋

History

Every calculation is saved with a chart of your recent BG and doses so you can spot patterns over time.

🔒

Fully Offline

No account required. No internet connection needed. Your data never leaves your device.

Important Disclaimer

⚠️ PocketDose is a calculation aid, not a medical device. PocketDose does not replace the guidance of your diabetes care team. The doses it calculates are based solely on the values you enter — always use your own judgement and consult your healthcare professional if you are unsure about any dose. Never use a calculated insulin dose if your blood glucose is below 4 mmol/L — treat the hypo first.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data we collect

PocketDose collects no personal data. The app does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not transmit any information to external servers.

Data stored on your device

The following information is stored locally on your iPhone only, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults):

  • Your insulin settings (type, concentration, ratios, target BG)
  • Saved meal presets
  • Dose calculation history (last 100 entries)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

Third-party services

PocketDose uses no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services.

Children

PocketDose does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
